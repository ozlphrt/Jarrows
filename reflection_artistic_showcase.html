Direction 1: Hero Studio

Philosophy: Never allow the user to look at a "dead angle". As the camera orbits, the light bank orbits in lockstep, guaranteeing consistent, flattering diagonal reflections on the blocks currently in focus.

Direction 2: Art Gallery

Philosophy: Ground the puzzle in a tangible, physical space. Multipane window reflection lines and an overhead skylight bar create a realistic sense of architectural grandeur as the tower turns.

Direction 3: Twin Light-Blade

Philosophy: Graphic, high-end automotive luxury. Double parallel white ribbons carve out the curvature of the block bevels like precision-machined sports car bodywork.
